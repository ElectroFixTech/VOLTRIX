Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how VOLTRIX ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you visit our website, create an account, place an order, or contact our support team.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the store, we may collect:

  • Account information: your name and the email address or account details provided when you sign in.
  • Order information: your name, Egyptian mobile number, governorate, delivery address, order items, quantities, total, selected payment method, and any notes you provide.
  • Support information: your name, email address, optional phone number, message category, and the message you send us.
  • Basic technical information: information needed for the website, account authentication, security, and reliable operation. Your browser or hosting providers may also process standard connection logs.

3. How we use information

We use this information to create and manage accounts, process and deliver orders, contact customers about their orders, respond to support requests, maintain store operations, prevent misuse, and meet applicable legal obligations.

4. WhatsApp and customer communications

If you choose to contact VOLTRIX through WhatsApp or use a WhatsApp order-contact feature, the information you send may be processed by WhatsApp/Meta under their own privacy terms. We may use the phone number and order details you provide to communicate with you about your request or order. We do not use this policy to promise that WhatsApp messages are end-to-end managed by VOLTRIX.

5. Payments

The store may offer Cash on Delivery and InstaPay as payment methods. Payment instructions or transfers through an external payment service are subject to that service's own terms and privacy policy. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, or full payment-card details through store messages or support forms.

6. Service providers and sharing

We use service providers that help operate the website, hosting, account authentication, and database services. Information may be processed by those providers only as needed to provide and secure the store, or disclosed when required by law. We do not publish customer contact details on the public storefront.

7. Data storage and security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. No website or online transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Access to customer information should be limited to people who need it to operate the store and provide support.

8. Data retention

We keep account, order, and support information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the store, handle customer service, resolve disputes, maintain business records, and comply with applicable requirements. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the reason it was collected.

9. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about the personal information associated with your account, request correction of inaccurate information, or request deletion where applicable. We may need to retain certain order or business records when required by law or for legitimate record-keeping purposes.

10. Children's privacy

The store is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate involvement of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate permission, contact us so we can review the request.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the store's features or practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

12. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, please use the Contact Us / Support section on the VOLTRIX website.
